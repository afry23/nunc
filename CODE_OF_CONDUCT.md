# Code of Conduct

nunc is a small project run in the open. Everyone who takes part – in issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews – is expected to keep it a respectful and constructive place, in English or German.

## Our standard

Expected:

- Be respectful and patient, also with people who are new to the project or to the topic.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people. Give feedback that helps the other person move forward.
- Assume good faith; ask before you assume.
- Accept that decisions about scope and architecture rest with the maintainers
  (see [`docs/adr/`](./docs/adr/)).

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as addresses, e-mail addresses or credentials.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, spam and unsolicited promotion.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, comments, releases) and to people representing the
project in public.

## Reporting

Please report unacceptable behaviour privately through GitHub:
[Security → Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/afry23/nunc/security/advisories/new). Use it for conduct
reports as well, choose the title "Code of Conduct" and describe what happened and where. The report is visible only
to the maintainers; names of reporters are kept confidential.

Reports about the maintainers themselves can be sent the same way; GitHub also accepts reports about content or
users via [github.com/contact/report-abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse).

## Enforcement

The maintainers review every report and may respond with a warning, remove comments, close or lock threads, or
block a user from the repository. Decisions are made in good faith and communicated to the people involved.
